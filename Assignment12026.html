Task 1

Q1. What is the medically relevant insight from the article?

A1. The paper presents a single-cell atlas of transcribed cis-regulatory elements (tCREs) across human tissues. Key medical insight: Many disease-associated genetic variants are located in non-coding regions. Identifying cell-type-specific regulatory elements can help explain how these variants influence gene expression and contribute to complex diseases. The atlas also shows that different cell types may use different regulatory elements to control the same gene, which may reveal disease mechanisms missed by gene-expression analysis alone.

Q2. Which genomics technology/ technologies were used?

A2. Single-cell 5′ RNA-seq: Main technology to identify transcription start sites and transcribed regulatory elements. scATAC-seq: To examine chromatin accessibility. Bulk RNA-seq and CAGE: To validate transcriptional activity and transcription start sites. Promoter-capture Hi-C: To investigate interactions between distal regulatory elements and promoters.

Q3. List and explain at least three questions/ hypotheses you can think of that extend the analysis presented in the paper.

A3. ・How do regulatory elements contribute to disease-associated genetic variants? ・How does cellular activation change regulatory-element activity? ・Do males and females have different regulatory mechanisms in disease-relevant cells?
